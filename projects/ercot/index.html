BEHIND THE DASHBOARD

Methodology & data notes

A public demonstration of data engineering and operational analytics, using ERCOT native hourly load data. The initial portfolio dataset covers January–September 2026; the validated dataset status above shows the current published measurement extent.

Source & reporting coverage

ERCOT provides the source load records. A separate pipeline validates them, stores measurements in PostgreSQL, calculates Python/SQL analytics, and publishes versioned JSON. Initial release: January–September 2026. Current measurement coverage appears in dataset status.

Demand, energy & regions

MW measures demand; MWh expresses energy from observed one-hour records. ERCOT system demand is its own reported series. Weather zones describe regional loads. Each zone's peak can occur at a different time; adding independent zone peaks does not produce the system peak.

Reporting dates & hour endings

Local hour-ending labels follow America/Chicago conventions. Hour ending 24 is midnight on the next calendar date but belongs to the prior reporting day. Repeated daylight-saving hours retain their occurrence number. Daily and monthly aggregates do not supply hourly curves or their own peak timestamps.

Quality, freshness & limits

The UTC export timestamp describes file generation, not new measurements. Missing values stay unavailable; matching counts alone cannot prove completeness. Energy uses observed records only. Monthly values do not change with daily filters. Weather observations, forecasts, and AI explanations are outside this demonstration.
