ABOUT THE FIRM

Benjamin Schleiger

Benjamin Schleiger is a utility-focused data engineer who builds reliable analytics systems for modern utility operations. His work spans SCADA analytics, Power BI dashboards, Python ingestion pipelines, and cloud-deployed machine learning systems.

At the City of Loveland, he designed a transformer-equipment database, consolidated SCADA and meter datasets, and built operational dashboards used daily by multiple utility divisions. He also engineered server-deployed ingestion scripts that refreshed SCADA data hourly, eliminating manual reporting.

At Pulse Fiber, Benjamin developed automated pipelines for MRR metrics and built forecasting models for long-range revenue projections.

He is currently completing his MS in Statistical Data Science at Texas A&M University, expected December 2027, and holds a BS in Computer Science from Texas A&M.
